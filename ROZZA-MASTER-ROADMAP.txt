ROZZA — MASTER PRODUCT ROADMAP V2
Goal: Build a world-class music product that feels unique, alive, intelligent, premium, fun, and unforgettable.

======================================================================
0. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
======================================================================

1. One tap should usually be enough.
2. Music should never randomly stop.
3. Never make the user fight the player.
4. Search should feel instant.
5. The queue should never become a dead end.
6. Arabic support must be first-class.
7. ROZZA should feel native, premium, alive, and fun.
8. Animations must improve the experience, not slow it down.
9. Every error should have a recovery path.
10. Playback reliability is more important than visual experiments.
11. ROZZA must have its own identity.
12. Build fewer features, but make them excellent.
13. Test playback-critical changes on a physical iPhone.
14. Make the app visually interesting enough that people want to keep looking at it.
15. ROZZA should feel like its own music platform, not another music player.

======================================================================

======================================================================

- Current YouTube foreground playback flow
- Existing YouTube state machine

- AVAudioSession setup
