# ROZZA app icon prompt

Create a premium original iOS music-app icon: a bold abstract rose whose petals subtly form flowing audio waveforms, with a play pulse at the center. Use a polished modern vector-like 3D mark, a near-black burgundy background, rich rose red, hot coral, restrained violet highlights, and a tiny teal accent. Center the emblem with generous safe padding. No words, letters, typography, musical-note symbol, headphones, copied logos, watermark, or rounded-corner mask.
